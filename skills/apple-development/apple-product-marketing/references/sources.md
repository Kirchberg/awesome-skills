## Current availability snapshot

The primary Apple pages supported these statements on the review date:

- [Custom Product Pages](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/custom-product-pages/)
  supported up to 70 additional versions for iPhone and iPad, localized
  creative, keyword assignment, unique URLs, and optional deep links with
  stated OS requirements.
- [What’s New on the App Store](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/whats-new/)
  marked product page headers, new search-result creative assets, Asset
  Library, and the new product-page preview as coming in fall 2026.
- [Manage App Tags](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-information/manage-app-tags/)
  limited display to the United States and described tags as based on
  `en_US` metadata, Apple systems, and human curation.
- [Accessibility Nutrition Labels](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-accessibility/overview-of-accessibility-nutrition-labels/)
  described initial reporting as voluntary, future submission requirements
  without a fixed deadline, and per-device common-task evaluation.

Recheck these pages at execution time. Record current, upcoming, limited
rollout, deprecated, or unavailable status rather than shortening all states to
“supported.”
