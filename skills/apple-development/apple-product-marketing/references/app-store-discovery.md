## Current-source gate

Before giving field limits, regional availability, feature counts, OS
requirements, or submission behavior, check current Apple documentation linked
from `sources.md`. Record the verification date.

As reviewed on 2026-07-28:

- Apple documents up to 70 additional Custom Product Pages for iPhone and iPad.
- Apple documents keyword assignment and unique search-intent combinations for
  Custom Product Pages, plus optional deep links with version requirements.
- App Tags are currently shown only in the United States and derive from
  `en_US` metadata, Apple systems, and human curation.
- Product page headers, new search-result creative assets, Asset Library, and
  the new product-page preview are announced for fall 2026 and must be treated
  as upcoming until live availability is rechecked.
- Accessibility Nutrition Labels begin voluntarily, are planned to become a
  submission requirement over time, and require common-task evidence per
  device before claiming support.

Do not preserve these statements as timeless constants. Recheck them when the
task depends on them.

## Discovery model

Separate these paths:

- **Search**: query relevance, accurate metadata, category and product context,
  ratings, promoted content, Custom Product Pages, and other current surfaces.
- **Browse and editorial**: category, collections, Today, Apps, Games, stories,
  events, meaningful updates, cultural relevance, and nominations.
- **Paid App Store acquisition**: Apple Ads placements and intent-matched
  product pages.
- **Referral**: campaign links, unique Custom Product Page URLs, website,
  social, email, earned media, and other approved channels.
- **Re-engagement**: In-App Events, promoted purchases, lifecycle
  communications, and relevant product destinations.

Do not claim that an observed correlation or third-party theory is a confirmed
ranking factor.

## Product-page audit

Inspect the actual page by storefront, locale, device, and app state:

1. Verify app name, subtitle, description, promotional text, keyword field,
   category, age rating, pricing context, privacy and accessibility information.
2. Verify the first three screenshots communicate the primary value without
   requiring the expanded gallery.
3. Check the complete screenshot and preview sequence against real product UI,
   current features, target device, and message hierarchy.
4. Check icon, ratings, review themes, developer responses, version history,
   In-App Events, promoted purchases, and available custom pages.
5. Check localization fallback and whether metadata, screenshots, and the
   binary support are being confused.
6. Check App Review Guideline 2.3 and current product-page and marketing
   guidance for accurate metadata and properly licensed terms and assets.
7. Record each finding with evidence, impact, confidence, owner, and a
   measurable remediation hypothesis.

Do not score a page from copy aesthetics alone. Connect each finding to intent,
conversion, trust, downstream quality, or compliance.

## Search-intent and metadata workflow

1. Build a semantic map from product research, reviews, support language,
   competitor alternatives, category language, search terms, and locale
   research.
2. Group terms by brand, category, problem, job, use case, feature, competitor,
   and seasonal or content intent.
3. Qualify every term by relevance, user intent, shipped product fit,
   storefront and locale, available demand evidence, competition evidence, and
   conversion quality.
4. Reject irrelevant volume, trademark misuse, competitor names where
   prohibited, price terms in keyword metadata, and repeated keyword stuffing.
5. Allocate concepts deliberately across name, subtitle, keywords, screenshot
   narrative, custom pages, events, promoted purchases, and ads according to
   current Apple rules.
6. Preserve readability and positioning. Do not turn customer-facing metadata
   into a comma-separated search-term dump.
7. Define a baseline and a measurement window before changing metadata.

Treat search-volume and difficulty scores from tools as vendor estimates.
Record the provider, market, date, and relative use; do not present them as
Apple measurements.

## Custom Product Pages

Create a custom page only for a meaningful intent or audience promise that can
be carried through to real content. For each page define:

- intent and audience;
- unique promise and proof;
- localized promotional text, screenshots, and previews;
- assigned keyword combination where currently supported;
- unique URL and channel;
- deep-linked destination and minimum OS behavior;
- review and publication status;
- primary conversion metric and downstream activation, retention, and proceeds
  guardrails.

Keep each keyword combination unique where Apple requires it. Maintain
continuity from search query or ad through custom page to destination. Compare
against the correct default page, storefront, cohort, and period.

## Additional discovery surfaces

- Choose primary and secondary categories from product function and user
  expectations, not chart competitiveness alone.
- Use In-App Events for timely, eligible experiences with accurate metadata,
  media, dates, audience, deep link, and re-engagement measurement.
- Promote in-app purchases only when the item, artwork, availability, and
  destination are accurate and current.
- Use pre-orders to test and accumulate demand only with a realistic readiness
  plan and current eligibility.
- Prepare featuring nominations around a meaningful launch, update, event,
  innovation, accessibility story, cultural moment, or developer narrative.
  Follow current lead-time guidance and never promise editorial selection.
- Audit App Tags for relevance where supported; improve truthful `en_US`
  metadata rather than attempting to manipulate generated tags.

## Ratings and reviews

Request ratings only after a natural moment of demonstrated value and use the
system review prompt according to current Apple guidance. Never gate content,
offer compensation, manipulate sentiment, or repeatedly interrupt a task.

For responses:

1. Identify sentiment, issue type, severity, locale, and whether account data
   is required.
2. Acknowledge the experience without exposing personal information.
3. Give only verified resolution or workaround information.
4. Move account-specific investigation to an approved private support channel.
5. Avoid arguing, soliciting a higher rating, or promising an unapproved fix.
6. Feed recurring themes into product, support, positioning, and experiment
   backlogs.

Measure review health with trends and themes, not rating averages alone.

## Policy guardrails

- Keep metadata and creatives accurate for the submitted and available build.
- Use only terms, trademarks, screenshots, testimonials, and comparative claims
  that the product is authorized to use.
- Keep the keyword field relevant and free of prohibited pricing, unrelated,
  or protected terms under current guidance.
- Do not fabricate reviews, awards, user counts, rankings, results, or
  accessibility support.
- Follow current App Store marketing identity rules for badges, Apple product
  images, naming, attribution, and minimum-space requirements.
- Escalate legal, regulated, health, financial, safety, privacy, children, and
  rights-sensitive claims to the appropriate reviewer.
