# Deliverables and readiness checks

Use these schemas to make outputs complete and reviewable. Select only the
artifact required by the request; do not emit every template by default.

## Contents

- Positioning brief
- App Store page audit
- ASO and intent plan
- Custom Product Page brief
- Localization brief
- Communication brief
- Launch plan
- Experiment plan
- Apple Ads plan
- Measurement plan
- Readiness checks

## App Store page audit

For each finding provide:

```text
Priority
Storefront, locale, device, and page
Element
Observed evidence
User, business, policy, or measurement impact
Verified rule or stated hypothesis
Recommended change
Metric and guardrail
Owner and validation
```

Audit name, subtitle, keywords, description, promotional text, category,
icon, screenshot and preview sequence, ratings and reviews, accessibility and
privacy context, localizations, events, promoted purchases, Custom Product
Pages, destination continuity, and analytics readiness.

End with no more than five prioritized hypotheses. Do not bury policy blockers
inside cosmetic suggestions.

## Readiness checks

Before saying **ready to execute**, verify:

- positioning and product promise are approved and evidence-backed;
- assets show the current available product and have necessary rights;
- metadata and keyword use pass current Apple policy;
- accessibility claims have per-device common-task evidence;
- storefront and locale research, glossary, and qualified review are complete;
- links, product pages, deep links, and fallbacks reach the intended state;
- current feature availability, roles, limits, and OS requirements are checked;
- analytics, campaign links, attribution, cohorts, and guardrails are ready
  before traffic starts;
- owners, review statuses, schedule, budget, support, pause, and rollback
  criteria are explicit;
- live App Store Connect edits, campaign spend, publishing, and user messages
  have separate authorization.

If a gate is missing, state “draft pending [specific validation]” and provide
the smallest next evidence needed.
